So i what do i like...let's say i love to learn how the world works, how does google know what we are searching for, how does instagram not run out of storage and how does it even manage all that data....random stuff

A bit of background: I was part of bi0s (India's No.1 competitive cybersecurity team). My focus there was forensics and DFIR analysis, and i somehow ended up playing 100+ CTFs and solving 150+ challenges along the way. The best one: we took **first place at the International Digital Forensic Challenge 2023** in Seoul, South Korea.

I've also won hackathons, including **FossHacks 2025** (hosted by FOSS United), where we built mARK.it, a hybrid bookmark manager, among 5000+ participants.

Right now i'm doing **Google Summer of Code 2026 with OpenWorm**, teaching a graph neural network to watch a *C. elegans* embryo develop cell by cell and predict what each cell grows up to be. It's live, so you can [go click a worm](/estgel/) yourself.

For scale: the adult worm runs on 302 neurons and roughly 7,000 synapses. My model needs 80,556 parameters to guess what those cells become, and still only gets 63%. The worm is winning on efficiency by a hilarious margin.

Worms are also excellent at humbling you. Early on my model scored 96% and i was thrilled for about a day, until i worked out that 96% of my labels were the same answer and it had simply learned to say "yes" to everything. I now refuse to believe a number until i've tried to break it.

I also work on Geospatial Data(Satellite and Lidar Imagery) using that for early warnings on natural disasters and climate forecasting as well(No need to fear of LandSlides Anymore!!!)

Somewhere in between i've written a few papers, on graph neural networks for fake news detection, music recommendation, behavioral cloning for self driving, and a book chapter on signal and image processing for healthcare and defense.

Other than those i have worked on various other projects around ESP32 sensors, GPS modules...made NIDS systems, a smart helmet that yells at you if you've been drinking, a leprosy detection app, and a lot of others, checkout my github to have a look
